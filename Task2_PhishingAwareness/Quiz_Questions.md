# Phishing Awareness Quiz (10 Questions)

Correct answers are marked with ✅. Copy these into a Google Form quiz.

**1. What is phishing?**
- A) A type of computer virus that deletes files
- B) A trick where attackers pretend to be someone trusted to steal information ✅
- C) A method to speed up your internet connection
- D) A firewall setting

**2. Which sender address is most likely a phishing email pretending to be "SecureBank"?**
- A) support@securebank.com
- B) alerts@securebank-verify.co ✅
- C) Both look safe
- D) Neither, sender addresses can't be faked

**3. You receive an email: "Your account will be closed in 24 hours unless you verify now." This is an example of:**
- A) Good customer service
- B) Urgency, a common social engineering tactic ✅
- C) A legal requirement
- D) Two-factor authentication

**4. In the URL https://securebank.com.login-verify.net/account, who really owns the website?**
- A) securebank.com
- B) login-verify.net ✅
- C) account
- D) https

**5. A website shows a padlock icon (HTTPS). What does this guarantee?**
- A) The website is owned by a trusted company
- B) The website cannot be a scam
- C) Only that the connection is encrypted ✅
- D) Your password will never be stolen

**6. What is "smishing"?**
- A) Phishing through SMS / text messages ✅
- B) Phishing through phone calls
- C) Phishing through QR codes
- D) A type of antivirus

**7. Someone on WhatsApp says: "I sent you a 6-digit code by mistake, please forward it." What should you do?**
- A) Forward it, it's harmless
- B) Ignore it and never share the code ✅
- C) Send it only if they seem friendly
- D) Post it in a group to ask others

**8. Your manager emails from a personal Gmail asking you to urgently buy gift cards and send the codes. What is the best action?**
- A) Buy them quickly so your manager isn't upset
- B) Reply to the email asking if it's real
- C) Call your manager on their known number to verify ✅
- D) Forward the email to your friends

**9. Which of these helps protect your account even if your password is stolen?**
- A) Using the same password everywhere
- B) Two-factor authentication (2FA) ✅
- C) Writing your password on a sticky note
- D) Turning off updates

**10. You clicked a phishing link and entered your password. What should you do FIRST?**
- A) Wait and see if anything happens
- B) Delete the email and forget about it
- C) Disconnect, then change your password from a safe device ✅
- D) Click the link again to check
